Here are some ground schools, news sources, podcasts, and study materials I've found useful as a pilot, plus some volunteer organizations on the West Coast that are worth looking into.

This list is always growing! Feel free to suggest anything I've missed.

## Ground schools

- [Flight Insight](https://www.flight-insight.com/)
- [Sporty's](https://courses.sportys.com/)
- [King Schools](https://kingschools.com/)
- [Gold Seal](https://groundschool.com/)
- [AviatorPro](https://aviatorpro.com/)

## News, current events

- [FLYING magazine](https://www.flyingmag.com/)
- [AOPA](https://aopa.org/news-and-media/news-by-topic)
- [General Aviation News](https://generalaviationnews.com/)
- [AeroTime](https://www.aerotime.aero/)
- [The Air Current](https://theaircurrent.com/)
- [AvBrief](https://avbrief.com/)

## Podcasts

- [The Reider Report](https://feeds.transistor.fm/reider-report) (Rob Reider)
- [Aviation News Talk](https://aviationnewstalk.com/) (Max Trescott at KPAO)

## Supplementary learning

- [CFI Commonplace Book](https://publish.obsidian.md/cfi-commonplace-book/~+Start+Here) put together by Jack O'Leary and other WVFC CFIs
- [Max Trescott's online courses on G1000 avionics](https://www.pilotlearning.com/)
- Seth Lake, DPE: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCAQS8w0x9swAMJRMhLY_P5g), [Substack](https://substack.com/@sethlakedpe)
- [Boldmethod](https://www.boldmethod.com/)
- AOPA Air Safety Institute: [Courses](https://www.aopa.org/training-and-safety), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AirSafetyInstitute)
- [FAASTeam and the WINGS program](https://www.faasafety.gov/)
- [PilotWorkshops](https://pilotworkshop.com/) (now part of Sporty's)
- [Brian Schiff's Neat Stuff](https://www.captainschiff.com/neat-stuff-links)
- [Useful Formulas for instrument flying](https://www.ryanfergusondpe.com/useful-formulas/) (Ryan Ferguson, DPE)

- [Urban Eagles](https://urbaneagles.org/)

- [Palo Alto Airport Association](https://paloaltoairport.org/)
- [Santa Monica Airport Association](https://www.santamonicaairport.info/)
- [Pilots N Paws](https://pilotsnpaws.org/)
